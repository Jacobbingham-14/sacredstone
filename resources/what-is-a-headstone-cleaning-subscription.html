What Is a Headstone Cleaning Subscription Service?

Last updated July 2026 · Sacred Stone Headstone Cleaning

Quick answer

A headstone cleaning subscription is a recurring plan — usually billed quarterly, semi-annually, or annually — where a service visits a specific grave marker on that schedule, cleans it using stone-safe methods, and sends a photo report afterward. It's built for ongoing upkeep rather than a single visit.

How it typically works

After signing up online and choosing a plan, the marker is added to the provider's regular route through that cemetery. Rather than being scheduled individually, it's grouped in with other subscribers in the same area and cleaned during a routine visit — which is part of how providers keep subscription pricing lower than one-off visits.

Why families choose a subscription over one-time cleaning

A single cleaning leaves a marker looking good for a while, but biological growth like algae and lichen returns steadily over the following months. A subscription keeps a marker consistently maintained without anyone needing to remember to book another visit — which matters especially for families who don't live near the cemetery.

Choosing the right frequency

Quarterly plans suit markers in shade or damp spots that collect growth quickly. Semi-annual plans balance cost and upkeep for most typical situations. Annual plans work well for well-drained, sunny plots that don't need frequent attention. Off-schedule rush visits are usually available as an add-on for specific dates like reunions or Memorial Day.
