How Often Should a Headstone Be Cleaned?

Last updated July 2026 · Sacred Stone Headstone Cleaning

Quick answer

Most headstones look their best with cleaning once or twice a year. Markers in shaded, damp, or north-facing spots collect algae and lichen faster and may benefit from quarterly attention, while sunnier, well-drained plots often stay presentable with just an annual visit.

What actually drives the timeline

Frequency depends less on a fixed schedule and more on a stone's specific environment. Shade slows evaporation and encourages moss and algae growth. Proximity to trees means more organic debris and root moisture nearby. Stone type matters too — porous materials like sandstone and older marble tend to show staining sooner than dense granite.

A general guide by situation

  • Sunny, open plot, granite marker: once a year is usually enough.
  • Partial shade or near trees: twice a year keeps ahead of biological buildup.
  • Full shade, damp ground, or older/porous stone: quarterly visits prevent heavier growth that's harder to remove later.

Catching lichen or algae early is also just easier — thin, fresh growth lifts with gentle cleaning, while growth left for years can require repeated treatments to fully clear.

Why a subscription makes this easier

Because the right frequency depends on the specific plot, a recurring plan is often simpler than trying to time individual visits yourself — especially for families who don't live nearby. Quarterly, semi-annual, and annual subscriptions each match a different level of upkeep without requiring you to track it.
