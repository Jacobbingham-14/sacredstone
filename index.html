Questions

Frequently asked

Will cleaning damage the headstone?

No. We use gentle, non-ionic cleaning solutions built specifically for cemetery monuments — the same standards used by preservation professionals. Nothing abrasive, nothing acidic.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions can be paused or canceled before your next billing cycle with no penalty, right from your own account portal.

Do you need the exact plot location?

It helps, but isn't required — we can often locate a grave using the cemetery, name, and approximate date if you don't have the section and lot number.

What if I just want one cleaning, not a subscription?

That's completely fine — one-time cleanings are available with no ongoing commitment.
