Headstone Cleaning in Orem, UT

Serving Orem from nearby Provo · Sacred Stone Headstone Cleaning

Service area

Sacred Stone cleans headstones at both Orem City Cemetery and Timpanogos Memorial Gardens — as a one-time visit or an ongoing quarterly, semi-annual, or annual subscription, with photo proof sent after every visit.

Cemeteries we serve in Orem

Orem City Cemetery sits centrally in the city and is known for its meticulous landscaping, serving Orem's long-established families alongside its growing community.

Timpanogos Memorial Gardens is a private cemetery tucked into a quiet part of Orem, offering a peaceful setting with views of Mount Timpanogos. It's included in our regular Orem route alongside the city cemetery.

Why a local, monthly route works well here

Rather than dispatching a special trip for every individual request, markers in Orem are grouped into our regular monthly cleaning route through both cemeteries — which is part of how we keep subscription pricing lower than one-off visits elsewhere.
