Headstone Cleaning in Provo, UT

Based in Provo · Sacred Stone Headstone Cleaning

Service area

Sacred Stone is based right here in Provo and cleans headstones at Provo City Cemetery and Eastlawn Memorial Hills — as a one-time visit or an ongoing quarterly, semi-annual, or annual subscription, with photo proof sent after every visit.

Cemeteries we serve in Provo

Provo City Cemetery is one of the largest and most historic cemeteries in the state, set in south Provo with views up toward the Wasatch Range. Its size and age mean markers here range from recent granite to older, more delicate stone — each cleaned with the method appropriate to it.

Eastlawn Memorial Hills, a private cemetery on Provo's northeast bench, offers sweeping views across the valley. It's included in our regular Provo route alongside the city cemetery.

Why local matters for this kind of service

Being based in Provo means no travel fee and no scheduling delay for markers within the city — your loved one's memorial is grouped into our regular monthly route through both cemeteries here, the same way it would be for a next-door neighbor's family plot.
