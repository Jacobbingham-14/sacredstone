## Intro / Trust Paragraph

Time, weather, and moss take a toll on even the most beloved memorials. Whether you live across town or across the country, Sacred Grounds Care keeps your family's headstone clean, legible, and cared for — on a schedule that fits your needs, with proof sent straight to your inbox.

We serve cemeteries throughout Utah County, including Provo, Orem, Springville, American Fork, and surrounding communities.

## How It Works

**1. Tell us who you're honoring**
Search by cemetery and name, or give us the section and plot number if you have it.

**2. Choose a plan**
A one-time cleaning, or a subscription that keeps their memorial cared for quarterly, twice a year, or once a year.

**3. We handle the rest**
Your grave is added to our monthly route. We visit, clean gently using stone-safe, non-damaging methods, and never rush the job.

**4. You get photos**
After every visit, we email you before-and-after photos — so you always know your loved one's memorial is being cared for, even from a distance.

## FAQ Preview

**Will cleaning damage the headstone?**
No. We use gentle, non-ionic cleaning solutions specifically designed for cemetery monuments — the same standards used by preservation professionals. Nothing abrasive, nothing acidic.

**Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
Yes. Subscriptions can be paused or canceled before your next billing cycle with no penalty.

**Do you need the exact plot location?**
It helps, but isn't required — we can often locate the grave using the cemetery, name, and approximate date if you don't have the section and lot number.

**What if I just want one cleaning, not a subscription?**
That's completely fine — one-time cleanings are available with no ongoing commitment.
