What's the Best Time of Year to Clean a Headstone in Utah?

Last updated July 2026 · Sacred Stone Headstone Cleaning

Quick answer

Late spring through early fall — roughly May through September — is the best window for headstone cleaning in Utah County. Temperatures are mild enough to avoid freeze-thaw stress on the stone, and cleaning solutions have time to dwell and work before winter weather arrives.

Why Utah's freeze-thaw cycle matters

Utah Valley sees real winter freezes, and any moisture that's worked its way into a hairline crack can expand as it freezes, widening the crack over the season. Cleaning right before a hard freeze — especially with methods involving extra water exposure — isn't ideal timing. A spring-through-fall cleaning gives the stone time to fully dry before temperatures drop.

Spring for a fresh start, fall for a clean finish

Many families clean in spring, both because winter moisture often leaves visible grime and because it lines up with Memorial Day visits. A fall cleaning is also worthwhile — it clears the year's biological growth before winter and gives markers a clean look heading into the holidays, when many families visit again.

Why Memorial Day books up early

Memorial Day is the single busiest time for cemetery visits in Utah County, and rush requests for a specific "clean it up before Memorial Day" visit spike accordingly. Booking a spring cleaning — or being on a standing subscription — well ahead of the holiday avoids the scramble.
