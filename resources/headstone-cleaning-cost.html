How Much Does Professional Headstone Cleaning Cost?

Last updated July 2026 · Sacred Stone Headstone Cleaning

Quick answer

A one-time cleaning typically costs $40-$75 for a flat or flush marker and $75-$125 for an upright headstone, with heavily soiled or marble monuments toward the higher end. Subscription plans — billed quarterly, semi-annually, or annually — generally range from about $150 to $400 a year depending on visit frequency.

What drives the price up or down

The biggest factors are the type of marker (a flat, flush marker is quicker to clean than a tall upright monument), the material, and the condition it's in. A stone with years of built-up lichen or algae takes longer and may need a repeat treatment, which shows up in the price. Larger family monuments with multiple names or a base plus an upright section also take more time than a single small marker.

One-time cleaning vs. subscription pricing

A single visit makes sense if you just want a marker looking its best for a specific date — a reunion, anniversary, or Memorial Day. A subscription is usually the better value if you want ongoing upkeep, since the recurring rate per visit tends to be lower than booking individual one-time cleanings throughout the year.

Rush requests

Providers who clean on a set monthly route, rather than dispatching individually for every request, can usually offer lower routine pricing — but a rush visit outside that schedule typically carries an added fee, since it means a special trip rather than being grouped in with other stops.
